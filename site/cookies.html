Cookies and storage

Updated 14 September 2026

No optional tracking

The Furl website code sets no cookies, writes no localStorage or sessionStorage, and includes no analytics or advertising trackers. Demo choices last only in the current page's memory and reset on reload.

Why there is no consent banner

There is no optional cookie or tracking category to accept. The site does not treat continued browsing as consent. If optional tracking is added later, it must remain off until an informed choice, with equally accessible accept and reject controls and a way to withdraw that choice.

Hosting and external links

Your browser may cache static files as part of normal HTTP delivery. Vercel may process technical requests for delivery and security. GitHub, PyPI and other linked websites have their own cookie policies when you visit them; they are not embedded here.

Read the privacy policy for technical data and support handling. Browser settings can clear cached site data. For questions, use Furl support.
